# 桃霞云岫测试记录

All included images are original native-imagegen outputs, generated once per prompt. The baseline and all six new examples measure 1672×941, close to but not mathematically exact 16:9. They have not been cropped, recolored or stretched. Actual prompts are included beside the corresponding images. No compiler was used.

The baseline demo02 received explicit positive aesthetic feedback. The six additional test cases were requested to broaden scene and shot coverage; permission to check them in does not mean each passed every visual criterion or received individual aesthetic approval.

## Baseline

The corridor, one nearby pavilion and the stone steps form a coherent inhabited setting. Residual issues: idealized soft facial texture, partly occluded route entrance and invisible feet. Preserve these limits when using it as a style reference.

1. 窗边倾听，facial detail: visible skin texture and color variation, coherent window light; nose and cheek highlights and texture emphasis may be stronger than desired. Hair crop follows the close framing, not a full-costume check.
2. 拈花回望，near: adult male stubble, fabric weave and hand placement readable; object is a large petal rather than a whole flower, and the pose remains somewhat portrait-like.
3. 卷帘纳光，medium: man adjusts bamboo blind within a coherent tea-room structure; face remains youthful/idealized and nearly profile, lower legs are outside frame.
4. 泊舟登岸，environmental medium: woman supported by rail, visible front shoe landing, boat/bank/stair relationship readable; rear foot is hidden and boat end of mooring is out of frame, so full support/mooring cannot be verified. Face remains soft and water area is broader than intended.
5. 春谷过桥，wide: small traveler, bridge, both banks and route form one valley; character around 9% frame height, larger than the prompt's 5–7% target. Foliage leans warm green. Skin and foot detail cannot be verified at this scale.
6. 沿坡归居，wide: two figures follow a legible path to one hillside dwelling; figures and house are larger than the stated prompt targets, foliage remains warm green and distant mountains are prominent. Back-facing distant figures cannot verify facial skin texture.

## Learning

Natural texture must remain age appropriate; adding visible pores alone does not prove realism. Richness works when it belongs to a coherent place, not when many landmarks compete. Close views need less environmental information; wide views require genuine scale changes. Retain scene-specific tradeoffs rather than turning one successful arrangement into a mechanical rule.
